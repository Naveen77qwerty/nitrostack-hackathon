# Enterprise Discount Policy (v2.0)
**Effective Date:** June 1, 2026  
**Department:** Finance Operations  
**Classification:** Internal  
**Document Owner:** Vice President of Sales & Chief Financial Officer  

---

## 1. Executive Overview

This document defines the authoritative discount parameters, approval authorities, and governance rules for all commercial transactions at Enterprise Global Inc. Supersedes version 1.0 (dated January 1, 2025).

---

## 2. Core Discount Limits & Fact Registry

The following authoritative policy values govern all enterprise contracts, pricing calculators, sales playbooks, and client proposals:

### 2.1 Maximum Discount Cap (`maximum_discount`)
- **Authoritative Fact:** `maximum_discount`
- **Value:** `10%`
- **Policy Statement:** Under no circumstances may standard commercial sales teams grant a cumulative discount exceeding **10%** of total contract value (TCV) without explicit CFO approval. (Previously 20% in v1.0).

### 2.2 Threshold Requiring Senior Approval (`approval_required_above`)
- **Authoritative Fact:** `approval_required_above`
- **Value:** `5%`
- **Policy Statement:** Discounts greater than **5%** require written authorization from the assigned Regional Sales Director prior to quote issuance. (Previously 10% in v1.0).

### 2.3 Exception Approval Authority (`discount_authority`)
- **Authoritative Fact:** `discount_authority`
- **Value:** `VP Sales`
- **Policy Statement:** Non-standard contract modifications or tier-3 discount approvals rest exclusively with the **VP Sales**. (Previously Regional Manager in v1.0).

---

## 3. Downstream Compliance Mandatory Audit

All sales enablement assets, proposal templates, customer-facing slide decks, and training SOPs referencing pricing must align strictly with the fact values above.
